No more [Opponent]

This overlay attempts to counter the "Opponent meta": using the paid perk to hide your username from your opponents. To do so, it makes use of the MCSR Ranked API to display it.

You can also have a few more stats displayed, if you wish... Or just keep it simple.

01 / PUBLIC STREAM

Make the match visible.

Link Twitch to your MCSR Ranked profile, make the connection public, enable Public Stream in Ranked settings, and go live. Either player's public stream can make the match visible.

02 / TOOLSCREEN

Give it a corner.

Add a browser overlay in Toolscreen and paste your generated URL. Enable Transparent and Refresh on Update. Use Top Right (Screen) with X −10, Y 215 for the usual opponent position. See sizes and settings ↗

03 / PLAY

Let the overlay follow.

It checks for your match automatically and disappears when the match leaves the feed. Updates are usually within about 10 seconds; API delays can take longer.

Why is my overlay empty?+

That's normal between visible matches. Check your username and make sure at least one player is streaming with Public Stream enabled. If the API is temporarily unavailable, the overlay retries automatically and may stay invisible until it recovers. A missing Twitch account or statistic won't stop the other fields from showing.

The public API may omit matches or player identities. Showing a name hidden in-game depends on what the API exposes.

The Purpose

Hopefully this acts as a "counter" to the "Opponent meta" and makes it no longer viable for top-level speedrunners to use that to hide their name. This will hopefully also make the MCSR Ranked team reconsider the paid perk of hiding your username to your opponents.
